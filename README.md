The primary analytical framing is **heritage governance, conservation readiness, community safeguards, and sustainable destination planning**. Tourism and destination development are applied domains, not predetermined objectives. The repository does not assume that tourism growth is desirable, that heritage should be converted into a tourism product, or that Spanish practices are transferable. The project may conclude that tourism development should be delayed, constrained, redirected, or not prioritized; it may also be modified or stopped.

This is an independent research and professional portfolio project academically aligned with Tourism Destination Planning and Management in Spain. It is not an official university project, thesis/TFM, commissioned study, government study, or institutionally endorsed work.

## Operating principles

- Development before growth.
- Evidence before interpretation; conservation before promotion.
- AI is a research assistant, never an evidentiary source.
- Announcements, plans, funding, implementation, and operational institutions are distinct statuses.
- Original evidence is preserved; translation, paraphrase, and interpretation remain distinguishable.
- Sensitive archaeological information is minimized and generalized by default.
- CHALUS remains an independent project and contributes nothing unless separately authorized by its own evidence boundaries.
- Publication follows **open methodology + selective responsible evidence publication**: public release may occur later, but sensitive, restricted, personal, and non-redistributable material remains protected.
